Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

27-0 is a free browser game available at 27-0.thecastpatrol.com.au, made by The Cast Patrol. We collect very little personal data, accounts are optional, and we do not sell your data.

Analytics
We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how the game is played, including pages viewed, device and browser type, approximate location, and in-game interactions (for example games started, seasons completed, share card opens). This data is collected automatically when you use the site. You can opt out at any time using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Advertising
Our games don’t show ads. If that ever changes, we’ll update this policy first.

Playing as a Guest
If you play without an account, we do not collect your name, email or any personally identifiable information. Your game history is saved in your own browser only. The one exception: after your first finished game, that game’s score and team (with no name or account) are sent once so we can show where it would rank on today’s leaderboard. We don’t keep it.

CAST Games Accounts (Optional)
You can create a free CAST Games account to back up your game history and see your stats across 27-0 and other Cast games. Accounts are optional and are for people aged 13 and over. You sign in with Google or Apple, and we never see or store your password.

What We Store
Your email address and a unique account ID from your sign-in provider (your email is held by our sign-in service, not stored with your game data, and only shared with our email provider if you opt in to emails; with Apple you can hide your real email). Your date of birth, used to confirm you are 13 or over and to keep betting advertising and partner offers away from anyone under 18. It is private to you and never shown publicly. Your email choice and when you made it. One opt-in covers CAST Games news and offers from our partners and sponsors (which can include betting partners); partner offers are only ever sent to adults 18+, so under-18s who opt in get CAST Games news only. Your favourite NRL team (or no team), your country, and your state if you are in Australia, which you give when you sign up and which place you on the club, country and state leaderboards. Your optional fan profile answers (gender, and whether you listen to The Cast Patrol podcast). Your game history and stats for each Cast game you play while signed in (for example mode, score, result and the players you picked), including games played on the same device before signing in.

Where It Is Stored and Who Can See It
Accounts use Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), with game data stored in Google’s Sydney, Australia region. Google processes this data on our behalf. Only you can see your data, apart from the leaderboard details below: our database rules only allow each account to read its own data. We never show your email publicly and we do not sell or share your personal data. We use combined, anonymous figures (for example the share of players who support each club) to understand our audience and show sponsors who plays; these never identify you. Your favourite team, location and podcast answer are sent to Google Analytics for reporting; your date of birth, gender, name and email are not. Your first name (from Google or Apple, or typed in by you) is kept by our sign-in service, shown only to you, and not stored with your game data.

Leaderboards
Every CAST Games account picks a username. It appears on the public CAST Games leaderboards next to your best scores and your favourite team's badge. Anyone viewing a leaderboard can tap your row to see the team behind that score: the players you picked and the result, nothing else. Nothing else about you is shown next to your name: never your real name, email, age or location. Your favourite team, country and (in Australia) state also place you on club, country and state leaderboards, so other players can see which of those boards you appear on. Usernames are checked for offensive words, and we may remove any that are offensive or impersonate someone. Scores are checked by our server before they are ranked. You can hide yourself from every leaderboard, or change your username, in your CAST Profile at any time. Deleting your account removes you, and those teams, from every leaderboard.

Friends and Challenges
If you add friends, they can see your username, team badge, ticks, your stats totals and most-picked players, your best leaderboard rankings and your last few games (with the teams you picked). They never see your email, date of birth or location. A challenge shares your result with the friend you send it to. Players can find you by searching your username, unless you hide yourself from the leaderboards. You can remove a friend at any time, and deleting your account removes you from every friends list.

Emails and Your Choices
We only email you if you tick the opt-in box. The same opt-in covers offers from our partners and sponsors, for adults 18+ only, sent by us: we never pass your email to partners. Every email has an unsubscribe link. We send emails with Kit (kit.com), our email provider. If you opt in, we send Kit your email address, first name, favourite team, state, country and podcast answer, plus which of our games you play, so we can send you relevant emails. Kit stores this in the United States and handles it on our behalf only. We never send Kit your date of birth, gender or game history. If you switch every email option off, we unsubscribe you in Kit straight away. If you delete your account we also unsubscribe you; email us if you want your record removed from Kit entirely. In your CAST Profile you can switch emails off, download a copy of your data, sign out, or permanently delete your account, game history and stats. We keep account data until you delete your account or ask us to.

CAST Pass Payments
If you buy CAST Pass, the payment is handled by Stripe (stripe.com), which receives your card details, your email address (for receipts) and your billing country under its own privacy policy. We never see or store your card number. We keep a small record with your account: whether you have a Pass, your plan, when it renews or ends, and the Stripe customer and subscription numbers that link it to your account. If you delete your account, we cancel the subscription and delete that record. Stripe keeps its own payment records as the law requires.

Hosting
27-0 is a static website. Standard technical information (IP addresses, browser info, request logs) may be processed by our hosting and CDN provider for security and performance purposes.

Changes
We may update this policy from time to time. The date above reflects the latest update.

Contact
Questions about this policy? Email us at cast@thecastpatrol.com.au.
