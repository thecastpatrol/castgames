In-Game Items

Players 15 and over can buy the in-game items below. Prices are in Australian dollars; The Cast Patrol is not registered for GST, so no GST is charged. Each is a one-off purchase through Stripe, not a subscription. Items go into the Vault on your CAST Games account, the same Vault in every CAST game, and stay there until you use them. They have no cash value and can’t be moved to another account. Nothing here limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.

Rerolls: A$0.99 each, 5 for A$2.99 or 12 for A$5.99. A Reroll gives you a fresh spin. One is only used once your free Rerolls for that season are gone.

Remove Players: A$0.99 each, 5 for A$2.99 or 12 for A$6.99. One takes a player out of your 13 so you can draft that spot again, and is only used once your free Remove Player is gone.

Reveals: A$0.99 each or 5 for A$2.99. In 27-0 a Reveal is Reveal Ratings: it shows the hidden ratings on the current squad. One is only used once your free Reveal is gone.

Superstar Keys: A$1.99 each or 3 for A$4.99; CAST Pass holders get 4 keys in a 3-key pack for the same price. A key makes your next spin in Classic or Pack include a player rated 95 or more. Your daily CAST Pass key is always used before a bought one.

Run It Back: A$0.99 each or 3 for A$1.99. One runs a finished Classic season back with one loss turned into a win: a lost Grand Final first, otherwise the closest regular-season loss. It can be used once a season. A season run back shows a boost mark on the leaderboards, and a run-back 27-0 counts as a Perfect Season. Run It Backs also keep a streak alive in the CAST over/under games.

Hard Mode Wildcards: in Hard there are no free wildcards for anyone, including CAST Pass holders. You can use one of each wildcard (Reroll, Reveal Ratings and Remove Player) per season, from your Vault or bought on the spot at the prices above.

Immortal Picks: when they are on sale, A$4.99 each or 3 for A$11.99. One puts an Immortal you choose from three into your 13 in Classic, Hard or Pack, rated 99. They also work in 1000TRIES.

88+ Picks: A$3.99 each or 3 for A$8.99. One puts a player rated 88 or more from the 2026 World Cup squads, chosen from three, into your 13 in World Cup 8-0, at his own rating. CAST Pass holders get one free, once.

World Legend Picks: A$4.99 each or 3 for A$11.99. One puts a World Legend you choose from three into your 13 in World Cup 8-0, at his World Cup rating. They also work in 1000TRIES World Cup 200+. CAST Pass holders get one free, once.

World Champions Picks: while World Champions runs (from the 2026 World Cup final until 11:59 pm Sunday 22 November 2026, Sydney time), A$5.99 each or 3 for A$14.99. One puts a World Champion you choose from three (from the final winner’s squad) into an open spot in your World Cup 8-0 13, rated 95 or more (the Player of the Final 99). CAST Pass holders get one free. Picks not used by the end of World Champions expire.

Promotion Picks: Team of the Year Picks (the 2026 Dally M promotion, until 8 am Saturday 3 October 2026, Sydney time) and Premiers Picks (the 2026 Premiers promotion) are sold only while their promotion runs, each A$3.99 or 3 for A$8.99. One puts a player from that promotion, chosen from three, into your 13 in Classic, Hard or Pack, rated 95 or more. CAST Pass holders get one free. Picks not used by the end of their promotion become 3 Superstar Keys each.

Picks are never used in the Daily Challenge, a friend’s challenge or a season run back. A season that uses one shows a boost mark on the leaderboards, and a season with an Immortal, Team of the Year or Premiers Pick can’t be sent as a challenge.

The GOAT and Legend Keys: the CAST Shop on 27-0 also sells the GOAT (A$9.99) and Legend Keys (A$1.99 each or 3 for A$4.99). They are used in the other CAST draft games, such as 1000TRIES, not in 27-0, and follow the terms on those games.

Streak Freezes: a Streak Freeze costs A$2.99. You can hold one at a time. If you miss a day, it is used automatically overnight so your CAST streak carries on. CAST Pass holders also get one free each month, on top of the one they hold and used before it. A Streak Freeze won from a reward while you already hold one comes as a Reroll instead.

Streak Restore: if your CAST streak of 3 days or more ends because you missed a day without a Streak Freeze, you are offered a Streak Restore for A$4.99 once, on your next visit. It brings your streak back as if the missed days were covered. The offer ends if you decline it, close it or play a game first.

Daily Challenge and Streak Rewards: completing a Daily Challenge, or every 10 days of a CAST streak, can earn free in-game items such as keys, Reveals, Run It Backs, Streak Freezes or Rerolls. Joining our emails for the first time earns one free Reveal, once per CAST Profile. They go into your Vault, have no cash value, can’t be moved to another account, and are only given for games played fairly. We may change or end rewards at any time without taking back items already given.

Invite Rewards: when a new player joins with your invite link and finishes a game, you both get 3 free Rerolls (players 15 and over, once per new player, up to 10 a month for each inviter).
