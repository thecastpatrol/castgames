How It Works

Three modes. One target: 100000 career points.

The Idea

Draft an NBA starting five from every era since 1946, plus the Legends. Every player carries his career NBA points, but you do not see the number until you pick him. Add up your team and try to reach 100000.

The Team

You fill 5 spots on the court: a point guard, a shooting guard, a small forward, a power forward and a centre. Each player plays one or two positions, the ones he really played over his career. If he fits more than one open spot, you choose where he goes.

HIGHER OR LOWER: NBA

A quick-fire mode of its own: you see one player’s career points and call whether the next player’s number is higher or lower, before the clock runs out. Play it with NBA points, rebounds or assists. Every right call adds one to your streak. One wrong call and you’re out.

Wildcards

  • Reroll (Respin in Team Spin): bin the current offer and get a fresh one.
  • Reveal Points: show the points for everyone on the current offer only.
  • Remove Player: take one player out of your team. His spot reopens and he goes back in the pool. If your team is full but short of the target, you can still use it before you lock in.

Wildcards come with a free CAST Profile: one of each per game, or two of each with CAST Pass. Guests see them locked until they sign in. Exactly 100000 is Hard Mode: no free wildcards for anyone, only paid ones, one of each a game.

Legends

Now and then in 100000+, one offer is a Legends Pick: three of the retired greats with 25000 or more NBA points, like Michael Jordan, Kareem Abdul-Jabbar and Kobe Bryant. You get one at most per game, and it can turn up late in a game, just when you need it most.

Legends keep their points hidden like everyone else, and they count their whole careers. They only appear in 100000+.

The Rules

  • Every player can only be picked once.
  • Picks are final, unless you use Remove Player.
  • You have 20 seconds to pick, or the first option goes in. That’s in 100000+ and the Daily Challenge. Exactly 100000 gives you 15 seconds, and the other modes have no clock. The clock waits while the Shop or a pop-up is open.
  • Your running total and how far you are from 100000 are always on screen.
  • Offers are random, but players with more points turn up a little more often.
  • Your result shows the luck of your draw: how many points were on offer, against an average game.
  • Team colours show the team each player last played for.
  • No bonuses, no chemistry, no tricks. Just points.

Where the Numbers Come From

Every point a player scored in the NBA regular season counts, over his whole career, including the BAA seasons from 1946 that the NBA counts as its own. Playoff games and ABA points are not included.

The pool is every player with 2000 or more NBA points, plus 21 Legends. Career points come from the NBA’s own stats at NBA.com, checked against Basketball-Reference, which also gives each player’s positions and teams by season. Up to date after the 2025-26 season.
