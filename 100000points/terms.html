Hard Mode Wildcards: Exactly 100000 is Hard Mode. There are no free wildcards for anyone, including CAST Pass holders. You can use one of each wildcard (Reroll, Reveal Points and Remove Player) per game, from your Vault or bought on the spot at the prices above.
