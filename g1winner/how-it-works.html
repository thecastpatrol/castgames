How It Works

Two draft modes, Photo Finish and a Daily Challenge. One target: 550 Group 1 wins.

The Idea

Draft 8 jockeys for an 8-race Group 1 card. Every jockey carries their Group 1 wins in Australia and New Zealand since 1983, but you do not see the number until you pick them. Add up your card and try to reach 550.

The Card

You fill 8 races on the track, Race 1 to Race 8. Every jockey can ride any race, so each pick goes straight into the next open race.

Photo Finish

Three lists to call: Jockey Group 1 Wins, Big Race Wins (the Melbourne Cup, Cox Plate, Caulfield Cup, Golden Slipper, Doncaster Mile, Stradbroke Handicap, Victoria Derby and Australian Derby) and Horses’ Group 1 Wins (every horse with 3 or more, from Winx down). All count Australia and New Zealand since 1983. One wrong call, or no call in time, and the run is over.

Wildcards

  • Reroll: bin the current offer and get a fresh one.
  • Reveal Wins: show the Group 1 wins for everyone on the current offer only.
  • Remove Jockey: take one jockey off your card. Their race reopens and they go back in the pool. If your card is full but short of the target, you can still use it before you lock in.

Wildcards come with a free CAST Profile: one of each per game, or two of each with CAST Pass. Guests see them locked until they sign in. Exactly 550 is Hard Mode: no free wildcards for anyone, only paid ones, one of each a game.

Legends

Now and then in 550+, one offer is a Legends Pick: three great retired jockeys, like Damien Oliver, Shane Dye and Glen Boss. You get one at most per game, and a player who has fallen well behind late in a game is sometimes thrown one as a lifeline.

Legends keep their wins hidden like everyone else, and they count their Group 1 wins since 1983 too. They only appear in 550+.

The Daily Challenge

A new challenge every day at midnight, Sydney time, with three goes and your best one counting. Sunday to Friday take turns through the decades and eras (the 1980s and 1990s, the 2000s, 2000 to 2012, the 2010s, Since 2013 and the 2020s: only jockeys who won a Group 1 in those years), Saturday is the Spring Carnival (only Group 1 wins in Melbourne count: Flemington, Caulfield, Moonee Valley and Sandown), and Sunday is the hard day.

The Rules

  • Every jockey can only be picked once.
  • Picks are final, unless you use Remove Jockey.
  • Your running total and how far you are from 550 are always on screen.
  • Offers are random, but jockeys with more Group 1 wins turn up a little more often.
  • Your result shows the luck of your draw: how many wins were on offer, against an average game.
  • Every jockey rides in the silks of their signature horse (James McDonald in Verry Elleegant’s, Hugh Bowman in Winx’s). A jockey whose silks we could not confirm rides in plain royal blue.
  • No bonuses, no form guide, no tricks. Just Group 1 wins.

Where the Numbers Come From

Wins count Group 1 flat races in Australia and New Zealand from 1 January 1983 to now, as graded at the time of each running. Wins overseas, jumps races and wins before 1983 are not included, and a dead heat counts as a win for both jockeys. Every jockey with 3 or more Group 1 wins since 1983 is in the game.
