Run Counts

Run and wicket counts in 60000 RUNS are compiled by The Cast Patrol: Test records from ESPNcricinfo’s Statsguru, names from Wikidata. They cover Test matches from the first Test in 1877 onwards. We work to keep them accurate, but they may differ from other sources and are provided without warranty. If you think a number is wrong, let us know.

In-Game Items

Players 15 and over can buy the in-game items below. Prices are in Australian dollars; The Cast Patrol is not registered for GST, so no GST is charged. Each is a one-off purchase through Stripe, not a subscription. Items go into the Vault on your CAST Games account, the same Vault in every CAST game, and stay there until you use them. They have no cash value and can’t be moved to another account. Nothing here limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.

Rerolls: A$0.99 each, 5 for A$2.99 or 12 for A$5.99. A Reroll gives you a fresh offer (a Respin in Team Spin), or a new player to call in Follow On. In a draft, one is only used once your free Rerolls for that game are gone. They work in every CAST game.

Remove Players: A$0.99 each, 5 for A$2.99 or 12 for A$6.99. One takes a player out of your XI so you can draft that spot again, and is only used once your free Remove Player for that game is gone. They work in every CAST draft game.

Reveals: A$0.99 each or 5 for A$2.99. In 60000 RUNS a Reveal is Reveal Runs: it shows the runs for everyone on the current offer. One is only used once your free Reveal for that game is gone. They work in every CAST draft game.

Legend Keys: A$1.99 each or 3 for A$4.99; CAST Pass holders get 4 keys in a 3-key pack for the same price. A key swaps an offer in 60000+ for a Legends Pick: three greats to choose from. Your daily CAST Pass key is always used before a bought one. They work in the main mode of every CAST draft game.

Hard Mode Wildcards: Exactly 60000 is Hard Mode. There are no free wildcards for anyone, including CAST Pass holders. You can use one of each wildcard (Reroll, Reveal Runs and Remove Player) per game, from your Vault or bought on the spot at the prices above.

The GOAT: A$9.99. It makes your next offer in 60000+ or Exactly 60000 this game’s GOAT, Don Bradman (6996 Test runs). Once per game, never in the Daily Challenge or a friend’s challenge, and only if he fits an open spot. A game that uses it shows a boost mark on the leaderboards. In another CAST draft game it brings that game’s own GOAT.

Run It Back: A$0.99 each or 3 for A$1.99. In Follow On, one lets you play on after a wrong call or a call out of time, keeping your streak. They work in every CAST over/under game.

Streak Freezes: a Streak Freeze costs A$2.99. You can hold one at a time. If you miss a day, it is used automatically overnight so your CAST streak carries on. CAST Pass holders also get one free each month, on top of the one they hold and used before it. A Streak Freeze won from a reward while you already hold one comes as a Reroll instead.

Streak Restore: if your CAST streak of 3 days or more ends because you missed a day without a Streak Freeze, you are offered a Streak Restore for A$4.99 once, on your next visit. It brings your streak back as if the missed days were covered. The offer ends if you decline it, close it or play a game first.

Daily Challenge and Streak Rewards: completing a Daily Challenge, or every 10 days of a CAST streak, can earn free in-game items such as keys, Reveals, Run It Backs, Streak Freezes or Rerolls. Joining our emails for the first time earns one free Reveal, once per CAST Profile. They go into your Vault, have no cash value, can’t be moved to another account, and are only given for games played fairly. We may change or end rewards at any time without taking back items already given.

Invite Rewards: when a new player joins with your invite link and finishes a game, you both get 3 free Rerolls (players 15 and over, once per new player, up to 10 a month for each inviter).
