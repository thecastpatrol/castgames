How It Works

Four modes. One target: 650 Premier League goals.

The Idea

Draft a Premier League XI. Every player carries their career Premier League goals, but you do not see the number until you pick them. Add up your XI and try to reach 650.

The Team

First you pick a formation: three of ten are offered, from a 4-3-3 or a 4-4-2 to a 3-5-2 or the diamond. Then you fill its 10 real spots, from left back to striker. There is no keeper. Each player plays up to two spots, the ones he was best known for. With a back four, a full back can fill the wing back spot on his side, and a winger and a wide midfielder can fill each other's spot on their side. With a back three, any wide player can fill a wide spot on his own side: a left back, left wing back, left mid or left winger on the left, and the same on the right. If a player fits more than one open spot, you choose where he goes.

Extra Time

Our over or under game, on its own page on CAST Games: you know one player’s career Premier League goals, assists or appearances. Is the next player’s number over or under? Call it before the clock runs out.

Wildcards

  • Reroll (Respin in Team Spin): bin the current offer and get a fresh one.
  • Reveal Goals: show the goals for everyone on the current offer only.
  • Remove Player: take one player out of your XI. Their spot reopens and they go back in the pool. If your XI is full but short of the target, you can still use it before you lock in.
  • Substitute, the Legend Key and The GOAT sit beside them for CAST Profiles, the same as in every CAST draft game.

Wildcards come with a free CAST Profile: one of each per game, or two of each with CAST Pass. Guests see them locked until they sign in. Exactly 650 is Hard Mode: no free wildcards for anyone, only paid ones, one of each a game.

Legends

Now and then in 650+, one offer is a Legends Pick: three of the Premier League’s retired greats. A Legend is a retired player with 120 or more Premier League goals, like Wayne Rooney, Thierry Henry and Frank Lampard. You get one at most per game, and it can turn up late in a game, just when you need it most.

Legends keep their goals hidden like everyone else, and they count their whole Premier League careers. They only appear in 650+.

The GOAT is Alan Shearer, the Premier League’s all-time top scorer with 260 goals. The GOAT item brings him straight into your XI.

The Rules

  • Every player can only be picked once.
  • Picks are final, unless you use Remove Player.
  • You have 20 seconds to pick, or the first option goes in. That’s in 650+ and the Daily Challenge. Exactly 650 gives you 15 seconds, and the other modes have no clock. The clock waits while the Shop or a pop-up is open.
  • Your running total and how far you are from 650 are always on screen.
  • Offers are random, but players with more Premier League goals turn up a little more often.
  • Your result shows the luck of your draw: how many goals were on offer, against an average game.
  • Club colours show the club each player last played for in the Premier League.
  • No bonuses, no chemistry, no tricks. Just goals.

Where the Numbers Come From

Goals count Premier League matches from 1992-93 to now. Goals in the old First Division, the Championship, cup competitions, Europe and for countries are not included. The pool is every outfield player who has played a Premier League game.

Career goals, assists and appearances come from the Premier League’s official records, checked against Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0). The numbers are up to date as of Matchweek 6 of the 2026-27 season. Positions are each player’s real ones.
