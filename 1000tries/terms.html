Try Counts

Try counts in 1000TRIES are compiled by The Cast Patrol from publicly available records and cover NRL premiership games from 1998 onwards (Legends and Immortals count their whole premiership careers). We work to keep them accurate, but they may differ from other sources and are provided without warranty. If you think a number is wrong, let us know.
