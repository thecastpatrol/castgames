How It Works

Spin, pick and build the biggest SC 13 you can. No target: the highest score wins, and the top of the board wears the crown.

The Idea

Every spin gives you three NRL player seasons from 2009 to 2026: the player, the year, the club he played for that year and his SC position or positions that year. Pick one for your 13. Every season carries the SC points he scored that year. You do not see them on the spin, but the moment you pick him, his SC points go up on his spot and your team total builds as you go.

The 13

You fill 13 spots in SC positions: a fullback (FLB), four centres or wingers (CTW), a five-eighth (5/8), a halfback (HFB), a hooker (HOK), two front rowers (FRF) and three second rowers (2RF). A season fits the SC position he had that year. If he had two that year, he can fill either, and you choose where he goes.

Hookers were listed as halfbacks in SC until 2015, so a hooker’s season from 2009 to 2014 goes in the halfback spot, and the hooker spot takes a season from 2015 on.

Wildcards

  • Reroll: spin again for three new seasons.
  • Reveal: see the SC points for all three seasons on the current spin before you pick. Reveals come from your Vault.
  • Remove Player: take one player out of your 13. His spot reopens and his season goes back in the pool. With your 13 full, you can still use it before you lock in.
  • Substitute (CAST Pass): move a player into an open spot in his other SC position that year.
  • Legend Key: swap a spin for a Legends Pick, three of the biggest SC seasons since 2009, with their SC points showing.
  • The GOAT: the biggest SC season of them all, Tom Trbojevic’s 2021, as your next pick.
  • SC Captain: once your 13 is complete, make one of them your Captain and his SC points count double. One per game, in Classic.

Reroll and Remove Player come with a free CAST Profile: one of each per game, or two of each with CAST Pass. Reveal, the Legend Key, the GOAT and the SC Captain come from your Vault, and the SC Captain is not part of CAST Pass. Guests see them locked until they sign in.

The Rules

  • One season per player: once a player is in your 13, none of his other seasons can join him.
  • You have 20 seconds to pick, or the first season that fits goes in. That’s in Classic and the Daily Challenge. The clock waits while the Shop or a pop-up is open.
  • Picks are final, unless you use Remove Player.
  • SC points stay hidden on the spin, unless a Reveal, a Legend Key or the GOAT shows them. Once you pick a season, his SC points show.
  • A game with an SC Captain shows a boost mark on the leaderboards.
  • Spins are random, but bigger seasons turn up a little more often.
  • Your result shows the luck of your spins: how many SC points were on offer, against an average game.
  • Each card shows the club he played for that season.

Where the Numbers Come From

Each season counts the SC points the player scored that NRL season, from 2009 to 2026, every season with at least one game. A player who changed clubs during a season wears the club he played the most games for.
